## Our Story

Madrid Bike Tours was founded in 2018 by two Madrid locals with a simple idea: the best way to truly experience a city is from the saddle of a bicycle.

We were tired of watching visitors shuffle through tourist traps or spend hours on crowded buses. Madrid deserves to be experienced at a human pace — stopping to smell the churros, chatting with locals, discovering the plaza that isn't in any guidebook.

So we grabbed some bikes, recruited the best storytellers we knew, and started pedaling.

## Our Guides

All our guides are born and raised in Madrid (or have lived here long enough to become madrilenhos at heart). They are passionate cyclists, history buffs, food lovers, and gifted storytellers. You won't just see Madrid — you'll understand it.

Our guides speak English, Spanish, French, and German.

## Our Bikes

We maintain a fleet of comfortable hybrid bikes suitable for all fitness levels. E-bikes are available on request (additional charge). All bikes are serviced weekly and come with:

- Adjustable saddle and handlebars
- Front basket for your belongings
- Puncture-resistant tyres
- Bell and front/rear lights

## Sustainability

We believe in responsible tourism. We offset our carbon footprint, partner with local businesses, and donate a portion of every booking to Madrid's urban cycling infrastructure projects.
