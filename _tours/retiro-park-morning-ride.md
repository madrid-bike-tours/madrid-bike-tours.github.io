## Overview

There is no better way to start a Madrid morning than cycling through El Retiro as the city slowly stirs. The light is golden, the paths are quiet, and the park feels like it belongs just to you.

This tour focuses entirely on Parque del Buen Retiro — 125 hectares of history, art, and greenery that was once the private gardens of the Spanish royal family.

## Highlights

- **Crystal Palace** — the iconic glass and iron pavilion from 1887
- **El Estanque Grande** — the majestic lake at the heart of the park
- **La Rosaleda** — the rose garden with over 4,000 varieties
- **The Fallen Angel** — one of the world's few statues of the Devil
- **Palacio de Velázquez** — another stunning exhibition pavilion
- **Secano Forest** — the wilder, wooded corner most visitors never reach

## Why Morning?

We specifically run this tour in the morning for two reasons: the light is spectacular for photos, and the park is peaceful. By midday, especially on weekends, Retiro fills with families and visitors. At 09:00 it's practically ours.

## Route Details

Completely flat. The park cycling path is fully paved and car-free on weekends. During the week, we use the quieter inner paths. All abilities welcome — if you can ride a bike, you can do this tour.

## Departure

Tours depart daily at **09:00** from the Puerta de Alcalá (by the Retiro main gate). The tour ends near Atocha station, making it easy to continue your day.
