## Overview

Madrid truly comes alive after 21:00. This evening tour combines a leisurely cycle through illuminated Madrid with stops at four of the city's best tapas bars — places our guides actually go on their days off.

We cycle through La Latina, Lavapiés, the Barrio de las Letras, and around the Palacio Real under the stars. In between, we stop for patatas bravas, jamón croquetas, fresh anchovies, and a glass of local wine or vermouth.

## The Tapas Trail

Each stop has been carefully selected for quality, authenticity, and a great atmosphere:

1. **Stop 1 — Vermouth in La Latina**: Traditional *vermutería* serving house vermouth from the barrel.
2. **Stop 2 — Croquetas & Jamón**: A beloved neighbourhood bar famous for its hand-made croquetas.
3. **Stop 3 — Seafood in Lavapiés**: Galician-style tapas in Madrid's most multicultural neighbourhood.
4. **Stop 4 — Dessert**: *Torrija* or *crema catalana* at a classic terrace café.

## About E-Bikes

This tour uses e-bikes. Not because the route is particularly hilly, but because cycling at night after a few tapas should be effortless and safe. The e-bikes are intuitive and require no prior experience — your guide will show you everything you need to know.
