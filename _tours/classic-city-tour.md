## Overview

Our Classic Madrid City Tour is the perfect introduction to Spain's vibrant capital. In three hours, you'll cover the highlights of the historic city centre and discover corners that most tourists never find.

We start at Plaza de Cibeles and wind our way through the Paseo del Prado, past the Royal Palace, through the maze of medieval lanes in La Latina, and finish with a stop for tapas in the Mercado de San Miguel area.

## Highlights

- **Plaza Mayor** — Madrid's grand central square
- **Royal Palace** — exterior and gardens (entry not included)
- **Almudena Cathedral** — the city's stunning neoclassical cathedral
- **La Latina** — medieval lanes and traditional taverns
- **Paseo del Prado** — the grand boulevard of the museums
- **Gran Vía** — Madrid's iconic Art Deco shopping street

## Route Details

The route is almost entirely flat and uses a mix of dedicated cycle lanes and low-traffic streets. No significant hills. The bike lane network in central Madrid has improved dramatically in recent years, making this route genuinely enjoyable for all fitness levels.

## Departure

Tours depart daily at **10:00** and **16:00** from Plaza de Cibeles (exact meeting point confirmed by email). Arrive 10 minutes early.

## What to Bring

- Comfortable, closed-toe shoes
- Sunscreen (especially April–October)
- A light jacket in cooler months
- Your curiosity!
