## Overview

The Madrid Río park is one of the most ambitious urban transformation projects in Europe. Where there were once six lanes of urban motorway along the Manzanares River, there is now a 10-kilometre car-free greenway connecting Madrid from north to south.

This tour takes you the full length of the park, with detours to the Royal Palace viewpoints, the historic Toledo Bridge, and the Isidro Meadows where Madrileños have picnicked since the Middle Ages.

## Highlights

- **Puente del Rey** — the King's Bridge with dramatic views of the Royal Palace
- **Parque de la Arganzuela** — futuristic bamboo pavilions and waterplay areas
- **Puente de Segovia** — Madrid's oldest bridge (1585)
- **Ermita de San Isidro** — chapel dedicated to Madrid's patron saint
- **Matadero Madrid** — a slaughterhouse turned contemporary arts centre
- **Legazpi neighbourhood** — a changing barrio with street art and craft breweries

## Route Details

The route is largely flat along the riverside path. There are a few gentle inclines at either end. At 18 km, this is our longest standard tour, but the pace is relaxed and there are plenty of stops. Suitable for anyone in moderate fitness.

## Departure

Tours depart at **10:00** from Puente del Rey (near the Royal Palace on the river level). The tour ends at the Legazpi end of the park, well connected by metro (line 3).
