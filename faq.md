## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do I need to be a strong cyclist?

No! All our standard tours are suitable for anyone who can ride a bike. We cycle at a relaxed pace with plenty of stops. E-bikes are available on request for those who want extra help on hills.

### What is included in the tour price?

All tours include: quality hybrid bike, helmet, guide, water, and a light snack. Some tours also include entrance fees to attractions — check individual tour descriptions.

### What happens if it rains?

Madrid enjoys over 300 sunny days a year! However, if there is heavy rain on the day of your tour, we offer a full reschedule or refund. Light rain is usually not a reason to cancel.

### What should I wear?

Comfortable, casual clothing suitable for cycling. Closed-toe shoes are required. In summer, bring sunscreen and a hat. In winter, dress in layers.

### Can children join the tours?

Yes! Children aged 8 and over are welcome on our standard tours. We also offer private family tours that can be tailored to younger children.

### How far in advance should I book?

We recommend booking at least 48 hours in advance, especially in peak season (May–October). Last-minute bookings are sometimes possible — contact us!

### Do you offer private tours?

Absolutely. Private tours can be completely customised — route, duration, language, and departure time. [Contact us](/contact/) for a quote.

### Where do tours start?

All tours meet at Plaza de Cibeles in the city centre. The exact meeting point is confirmed in your booking email.

### Is there a luggage storage option?

We don't provide luggage storage, but there are several luggage storage services near our meeting point. We recommend travelling light!
